BOLD DRAGOON CC — SUPABASE NEXT STEPS

Do these only after Version 12.1 is live and the app buttons work.

1. Open your Supabase project.
2. Open SQL Editor.
3. Click New query.
4. Open SUPABASE_SETUP.sql from this folder.
5. Copy all of it into Supabase and click Run.
6. Confirm it says Success.
7. Open a second New query.
8. Open SUPABASE_SECURE_AUTH.sql.
9. Copy all of it into Supabase and click Run.
10. Go to Authentication > Providers and enable Email.
11. Open the app and register your own account.
12. In SQL Editor run:

update public.club_profiles
set approved = true, role = 'committee'
where email = 'REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_EMAIL';

13. Refresh the app and sign in.

Do not put the Supabase service-role key in GitHub or Netlify.
The existing Netlify environment variables should only use:
- VITE_SUPABASE_URL
- VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY
